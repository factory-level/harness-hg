# The avatar inventory (#426, #635, ADR-146)

Role-named brand art: isometric pixel-diorama animations, one per business
role, grouped by domain color family (#636 — domain first, job title second:
SRE teal, Marketing purple/coral, Software indigo, Business warm gray/gold,
Finance green, Legal burgundy, Events amber, C-Suite black, Sales crimson,
HR rose). Produced from reference-anchored stills → locked-camera Higgsfield
videos → 128×128 animated WebP with real 8-bit alpha, keyed by
`infra/scripts/rekey-avatars.py`, whose mask is the UNION of a semantic matte
(`image_background_remover` on the median frame, 1 credit per avatar — keeps
dark hair and suits) and a geometric border flood (keeps the stone floor the
matte fades out). Run it with `--sources <dir of <role>.mp4> --mattes <dir of
<role>.png> --out control-plane/nexus/avatars`.
WebP replaced GIF's 1-bit transparency because hard on/off edge pixels
fringe over light backgrounds; the pipeline is committed because its two
predecessors were not, and re-keying meant reverse-engineering commit
messages.

The serving contract (enforced by `avatar_asset_error` in
`control-plane/nexus/plugin_api.py`, swept by
`plugin/tests/test_nexus_plugin_api.py`): animated GIF or VP8X WebP,
≤512KB, ≤256×256, square. An agent selects one by putting the file stem
(e.g. `sre-seated`) in its `display.icon` declaration; the `#/avatars`
gallery in Nexus shows the codes. Replace art by replacing its file —
same stem, same contract.

Each role also ships a `<role>-still.webp`: the animation's first frame.
It serves reduced-motion users, and every non-canvas surface (the agents
directory) requests it always — the canvas is the only animating surface
(ADR-146). Ship both files or the pytest sweep fails the commit. The still
is a bare lossless still rewrapped in the VP8X container, because the
serve gate requires that layout and encoders collapse stills out of it.

Emits carry only the ids agents actually wear plus their `-still` twins
(`cli/src/nexus/emit.ts`, ADR-146) — the full library lives here, not in
every gitops repo.

The retired `bot-*` placeholder generator (`infra/scripts/gen-avatars.py`)
remains for making new dev-time placeholders; nothing here is generated by
it anymore.
