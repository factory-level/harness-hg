# The other views

**What this page tells you:** the views beside the canvas, and the two habits that make any
of them safe to read.

The Fleet Canvas is where you arrange things. These are where you read them. All are
read-only. Each shows a summary that fits on a screen, with detail one click away.

| View | Shows | Deep link |
|---|---|---|
| [Alert Routing](alert-routing.md) | what is firing, and whether deliveries get through | `#/communication` |
| [Agents](agents.md) | every agent, its state, its attachments | `#/agents` |
| [Backups](backups.md) | what protects each installed unit, and what nothing protects | `#/backups` |
| [System](system.md) | the control plane as a ring of capabilities | `#/system` |
| [Avatars](avatars.md) | the avatar inventory | `#/avatars` |

## Two habits

**`unknown` is not `healthy`.** A blank is a question, not an answer.

**Demo builds say so.** A Nexus UI with no repository configured shows fixture data and a
`DEMO DATA` badge. The screenshots on these pages were taken that way.
