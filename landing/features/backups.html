Backups

Preserve what the fleet knows.

An agent’s useful state outgrows its declaration the moment it starts working. Every profile ships a backup schedule with its record, and restore is a rehearsal, not a hope.

Why it matters

Reproducible from Git is only half the fleet.

The declarations rebuild the agents, but not what the agents have learned, collected or written. Backups carry that other half — on a schedule declared in the same record that deploys the agent, captured into snapshots a restore can actually use.

The devlog records why rehearsal is the point: the first honest run of the recovery gate failed on a credential someone had created by hand, weeks earlier, while every dashboard stayed green.

Everything was green the entire time.

— from the devlog

How it fits

From schedule to proven restore.

  1. 01

    The record declares the schedule.

    Backup cadence and retention ship with the profile, not beside it.

  2. 02

    State lands in restorable snapshots.

    Each agent’s working state is captured on its own schedule.

  3. 03

    Recovery is proven, not presumed.

    The recovery gate rebuilds from Git plus snapshots and reports pass, fail or unknown — never a silent pass.
