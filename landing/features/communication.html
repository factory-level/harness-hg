Communication

Agents that say what happened.

Agents publish events and register for the ones they need. Inbound and outbound are first-class and named — the event surface is part of the declaration, not a side effect of the code.

Why it matters

Integrations nobody named are integrations nobody reviews.

Point-to-point integrations rot quietly: nothing names them, so nothing reviews them, and the day one breaks is the day you learn it existed. A declared event surface makes the connections between agents — and to the outside — visible, reviewable and diffable like everything else in the record.

On the canvas, every event is a readout with its registrations listed beneath the event it belongs to.

You can see who is listening before you change what a publisher emits.

How it fits

From declaration to delivery.

  1. 01

    Declarations name the events.

    What an agent publishes and what it registers for are fields, not folklore.

  2. 02

    The event router carries them.

    A durable transport moves events between agents and external endpoints.

  3. 03

    The canvas shows the surface.

    Inbound and outbound readouts list every registration, per event.
