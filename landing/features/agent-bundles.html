Agent bundles

Install the whole team.

A bundle runs several agents in one shared runtime pod. Their Agent Team Repo declares the agents, supporting apps and required secrets. Share that repository to install the team elsewhere.

Why it matters

Teams deploy together, or they drift apart.

Agents that work together share infrastructure — a database, a runbook store, the events that connect them. Installed one at a time, those relationships live in someone’s head and are rebuilt by hand in every new environment. A bundle keeps the team and its relationships in one declaration, so what deploys together is declared together.

Members of a bundle share a boundary, and the devlog records how that asymmetry was found. Choose bundles for teams that belong together; keep agents separate when isolation matters.

Bundling is a co-location decision, not a security one.

— from the devlog

How it fits

From one declaration to one Application.

  1. 01

    Declare the bundle and its members.

    One declaration names the agents, the apps they run beside, and what each needs.

  2. 02

    Harness Hg renders the deployment record.

    The bundle renders as one record in Git — reviewable, reproducible, reversible by revert.

  3. 03

    Argo CD deploys it as one unit.

    Argo CD deploys the bundle’s shared runtime. Supporting apps and declared backup routines are rendered from the team’s declarations.
