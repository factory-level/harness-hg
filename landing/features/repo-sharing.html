Repository sharing

Take the team with you.

Capabilities live in repositories: a persona repository declares agents and what they need, a distribution versions the offering, and any environment installs it with one command.

Why it matters

A capability trapped in one repo gets rebuilt, not reused.

Every rebuild drifts: the second copy of a team is never quite the first, and the differences surface at the worst time. When the declaration is the shareable unit, moving a team between repositories, machines or people is an install — pinned by ref and sha, reproducible on arrival.

Sharing flows one way. The platform defines the contract, persona repositories adopt it, and validation runs against the declarations before anything reaches a cluster.

An install, not an archaeology dig.
