Build log

The devlog

One person building a deployment platform for AI agents, in the open. These are the design calls — the ones that held, the ones that were wrong, and the ones still unresolved. Every entry links the decision record it came from and the code that proves it.

3 Aug 2026 Bootstrap · ADR-63
The copy is always the one that rots One version pin had quietly reached three copies across two languages. The fix was not discipline or a checklist — it was making the second copy fail the build.
2 Aug 2026 Recovery · ADR-52
The rehearsal proved the fleet was not rebuildable The first honest run of the recovery gate failed at the rebuild, on a credential someone had created by hand weeks earlier. The fleet had not been rebuildable since that day — and everything was green the entire time.
2 Aug 2026 Nexus · ADR-44
The panel rendered perfectly and meant something else I built the first embedded dashboard, verified it in a browser, and then withdrew it. Nothing about the frame was broken. The tab said Uptime and the panel behind it was spend.
1 Aug 2026 Bundles
Bundling is a co-location decision, not a security one The same asymmetry turned up three separate times before I named it. Moving two agents into a bundle to save resources was quietly removing the ability to isolate them.

Nineteen days old at the time of writing. The whole decision log is published, and so is every place the system falls short of its own design.
